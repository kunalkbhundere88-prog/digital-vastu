Five Elements of Vastu

Pancha Mahabhutas

According to traditional Vastu Shastra, the five basic elements are Earth, Water, Fire, Air and Space. These are known as the Pancha Mahabhutas.

🌍 Earth - Prithvi

Earth represents stability, strength and balance. It is traditionally associated with the Southwest direction.

💧 Water - Jal

Water represents flow, purity and calmness. It is traditionally associated with the Northeast direction.

🔥 Fire - Agni

Fire represents energy, transformation and activity. It is traditionally associated with the Southeast direction.

💨 Air - Vayu

Air represents movement, communication and freshness. It is traditionally associated with the Northwest direction.

🌌 Space - Akash

Space represents openness and the area in which the other elements exist. It is traditionally associated with the center of a home.
